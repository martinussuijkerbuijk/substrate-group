## Rent Structure

IDEMIA exemplifies the **intellectual-property rentier** model identified by Christophers: the company holds proprietary biometric algorithm IP and charges recurring license fees for matching software, enrollment devices, and secure document production. Public-sector clients (including EU agencies) are locked into perpetual upgrade cycles because biometric systems require continuous algorithm retraining and device recertification.

**Confidence:** Inferred — Based on industry-standard contract structures and Christophers' rentier framework applied to biometric IP
