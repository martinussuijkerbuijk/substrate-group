# Entity: eu-LISA

**Full Name:** European Agency for the operational management of large-scale IT systems in the area of freedom, security and justice  
**Established:** 2012 (Regulation (EU) No 1077/2011)  
**Headquarters:** Tallinn, Estonia  
**Legal Form:** EU decentralised agency  
**Governing Law:** EU law; specific instruments for each system  
**Confidence:** Documented — EU Official Journal, agency statutes publicly available  
**Temporal Referent:** Established 2012; mandate expanded repeatedly (2016, 2018, 2021)

---

## Role in Border Biometric Infrastructure

Operational manager for:
- **Eurodac** (asylum applicant fingerprint database) — since 2013
- **VIS** (Visa Information System) — since 2014
- **SIS II** (Schengen Information System) — since 2014
- **EES** (Entry/Exit System) — deployment initiated, repeatedly delayed; central infrastructure contract awarded ~2021
- **ETIAS** (European Travel Information and Authorisation System) — under development

**Confidence:** Documented — eu-LISA annual activity reports, EU regulations

---

## Procurement Authority

eu-LISA acts as contracting authority for central system development, maintenance, and hosting. It operates under EU public procurement rules (Directives 2014/24/EU, 2014/25/EU) but frequently invokes Article 346 TFEU and security exemptions to limit competitive tendering and transparency.

**Confidence:** Corroborated — EU procurement directives; consistent pattern in eu-LISA annual reports noting "negotiated procedures" for security-sensitive contracts
