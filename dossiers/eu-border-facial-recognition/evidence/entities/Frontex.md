# Entity: Frontex

**Full Name:** European Border and Coast Guard Agency  
**Established:** 2004 (European Agency for the Management of Operational Cooperation at the External Borders); renamed and mandate expanded 2016  
**Headquarters:** Warsaw, Poland  
**Legal Form:** EU decentralised agency  
**Governing Law:** EU law; Regulation (EU) 2019/1896 (current mandate)  
**Confidence:** Documented — EU Official Journal  
**Temporal Referent:** Established 2004; renamed 2016; mandate expanded 2019

---

## Role in Border Biometric Infrastructure

- Operational coordination of border surveillance and returns
- Risk analysis and situational awareness
- Procurement of its own equipment, including mobile biometric devices and aerial surveillance
- **Not** the operational manager of EES/ETIAS — that is eu-LISA — but heavily involved in operational requirements and risk-analysis data flows

**Confidence:** Documented — Frontex regulation and eu-LISA-Frontex working arrangements

---

## Procurement Patterns

Frontex operates a separate procurement stream from eu-LISA, purchasing:
- Aerial surveillance services (drones, manned aircraft)
- Mobile biometric identification units
- Vessel chartering and maritime equipment
- IT services for risk analysis

Frontex has faced repeated criticism from the European Ombudsman and Court of Auditors for procurement opacity, including framework contracts with limited competitive tendering and inadequate subcontractor disclosure.

**Confidence:** Corroborated — European Ombudsman decisions (e.g., 2020/2021), European Court of Auditors reports
