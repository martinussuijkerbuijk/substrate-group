## Rent Structure

NEC represents **IP rent** in its purest form: the company owns proprietary facial recognition algorithm IP (developed over decades, protected by patents) and licenses it to systems integrators and end-users. Unlike IDEMIA or Thales, NEC does not typically manufacture document hardware; it extracts rent from software licenses and algorithm performance.

**Confidence:** Inferred — Based on NEC's public business model descriptions and Christophers' IP rent framework
