## Rent Structure

Thales/Gemalto combination created a **chokepoint rentier** in secure document and biometric verification markets. EU Member States cannot easily switch ePassport or national ID card suppliers because:
1. Document security features are proprietary and certified per vendor
2. ICAO and EU standards are implemented through vendor-specific toolkits
3. Switching costs include re-enrollment of entire populations and re-certification of border hardware

This is contract rent and IP rent combined: the public pays recurring fees for document personalization, key management, and algorithm updates.

**Confidence:** Inferred — Based on industry structure and Christophers' rentier framework; specific contract terms not publicly available
