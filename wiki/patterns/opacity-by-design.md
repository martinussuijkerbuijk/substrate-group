# Pattern: Opacity by Design

**Definition:** A recurring pattern in AI infrastructure procurement and deployment where opacity is an engineered feature serving specific interests, not an incidental limitation. Distinct from mere complexity.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- Contractual NDA clauses covering system performance data
- Jurisdictional structure that places accountability in multiple legal regimes, none of which has full authority
- Algorithmic complexity deployed beyond what task performance requires (obfuscation surplus)
- Impact assessments that aggregate results at a scale that dissolves individual harm
- Procurement processes structured to prevent competitive bidding on successor contracts (lock-in as opacity)
- Audit rights granted on paper but operationally unreachable

**How to document it:**
1. Establish what *would* be disclosed if transparency were the default
2. Map the specific mechanism by which each disclosure is blocked
3. Identify who benefits from each specific opacity (not "the company" in general)
4. Produce a structural diagram: the opacity architecture as a finding
