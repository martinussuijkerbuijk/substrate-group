# Pattern: Regulatory Capture Through Complexity

**Definition:** The deliberate production of technical and legal complexity at a scale that exceeds the capacity of any regulator, auditor, or affected community to comprehend, creating de facto impunity while maintaining formal compliance structures.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- Technical documentation that is formally complete but operationally unreadable by non-specialists
- Regulatory frameworks that require individual harm to be demonstrated when harm is structural
- Standard-setting processes dominated by industry without community representation
- Audit frameworks designed and implemented by the audited party
- "Explainability" requirements that produce explanations too complex to act on

**Distinction from straightforward capture:** Classic regulatory capture works through revolving door relationships and political donations. This pattern works through *epistemic* capture — the regulator cannot understand what they are regulating well enough to regulate it. The harm is structural, not corrupt.

**Counter-move:** The Counter-Forensic Investigator's role includes producing a "translation" — an account of the system in terms a specific regulatory forum can understand and act upon. This is the methodology designer's brief: what form must this evidence take to be usable by this specific institution?
