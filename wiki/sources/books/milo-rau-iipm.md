# (Re)Creation Processes: Milo Rau and the IIPM — Lily Climenhaga (PhD thesis, 2021)

**Argument:** Milo Rau's work with the International Institute of Political Murder (founded 2008) cannot be reduced to "reenactment theatre." Climenhaga identifies the distinctive organizational and aesthetic categories structuring IIPM work across 2008–2020: a documentary-political mode that recreates real events (trials, massacres, electoral processes) on stage to produce new political meaning. The thesis argues for Rau as developing a practice where the act of recreation itself becomes the political intervention.

**Why it matters to us:** Rau's method is one of the team's procedural ancestors for how to make evidence *visible*. The theatrical reenactment of a trial (e.g., *The Congo Tribunal*) is structurally analogous to the team's dossier practice: assembling evidence into a form that makes harm legible to new audiences. Also relevant to Methodology Designer: what does it mean to transfer investigative findings into public-facing formats that create accountability without simplifying complexity?
