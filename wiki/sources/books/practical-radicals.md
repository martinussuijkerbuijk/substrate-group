# Practical Radicals — Deepak Bhargava & Stephanie Luce

**Argument:** Transformational change from below requires strategy, not just moral clarity. Bhargava and Luce analyze seven strategy models for "underdogs" — those without institutional power — and argue that vision, conjuncture analysis, and power-mapping are prerequisites for any effective campaign. The book synthesizes decades of US social movement experience into transferable strategic frameworks.

**Why it matters to us:** The Methodology Designer and Community Liaison are the primary users. The team's investigations produce evidentiary resources — but evidentiary resources without a theory of how they enter political and social circulation are inert. Practical Radicals provides that theory: what conditions make evidence actionable? Who needs to receive it, in what form, through what channels, at what moment in a campaign cycle?
