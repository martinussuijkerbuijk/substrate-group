# The Art of Resistance — Bläske, Casella, Rau, Staal (eds.) (2020)

**Argument:** Theatre, activism, and solidarity are not separate domains — they are modes of a single practice of resistance against imperialism and neoliberal capitalism. This NTGent Golden Book IV gathers speeches, essays, interviews, and manifestos from artists, activists, journalists, and lawyers. Rau's essay "Reality comes from Utopia" argues that artistic practice oriented toward solidarity must operate from a utopian horizon, not merely reactive critique.

**Why it matters to us:** The Methodology Designer and Community Liaison use this for the public-facing dimension of the team's work. How do investigation findings move from dossier to public? The contributors here — working at the intersection of theatre, law, and activism — demonstrate practices for making structural analysis emotionally and politically legible to non-expert audiences. The format itself is instructive: essays + manifestos + interviews is a multi-format evidence presentation, which is what the Open Dossier should aspire to.
