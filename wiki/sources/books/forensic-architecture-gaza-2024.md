# Forensic Architecture — Spatial Analysis of IDF Conduct in Gaza (October 2024)

**Argument:** This is an active investigation report, not a theory text. Forensic Architecture documents the spatial logic of Israeli military operations in Gaza since October 2023 across seven analytical domains: spatial control, displacement, destruction of agriculture and water resources, destruction of medical infrastructure, destruction of civilian infrastructure, targeting of aid, and a cross-sectional analysis. The report presents spatial evidence for legal and paralegal forums.

**Why it matters to us:** This is FA's methods *in practice* at maximum scale. Reading it alongside [[forensic-architecture-violence]] shows how the theoretical framework operationalizes into a 900-page evidentiary document. Methodologically: note how FA handles scale (it investigates spatial patterns, not individual incidents), how it structures confidence levels, and how it produces evidence designed for specific forums (ICJ, ICC, UN mechanisms). The cross-sectional analysis is the equivalent of our Shape of the Gap — what does the *pattern across* all evidence layers reveal that no single layer could?
