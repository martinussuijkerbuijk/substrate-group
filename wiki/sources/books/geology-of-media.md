# A Geology of Media — Jussi Parikka (2015)

**Argument:** Digital media is geological. The minerals extracted for electronic devices, the waste landscapes of obsolete hardware, the energy of computation — these connect media to the deep time of the earth. Parikka develops "media archaeology" into a materialist, ecological critique: media systems are not cultural objects that happen to have a material base; they *are* material processes operating across geological time. The "Anthrobscene" names this condition — the obscenity of the Anthropocene as media event.

**Why it matters to us:** Grounds the Material pillar philosophically. The supply chains the Material Ecologist maps are not just logistics — they are geological processes in the Parikka sense. This is why the mineral origins of semiconductors and the energy of data centers are primary evidence, not background context. Also connects to the Stiegler argument: the technical object is geological before it is cultural.
