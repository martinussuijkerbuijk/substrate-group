# Atlas of AI — Kate Crawford (2021)

**Argument:** AI is not an immaterial, cloud-based intelligence — it is a planetary extractive industry. Crawford maps AI across its material substrates: the earth (lithium, cobalt), labor (Amazon warehouse workers, data labelers), data (mass surveillance and classification), affect (emotion recognition systems), and state power (military and policing applications). AI systems concentrate power and reproduce existing social hierarchies at scale.

**Why it matters to us:** This is the foundational empirical atlas for the Material and Capital pillars. Crawford's chapter structure maps almost exactly onto the team's four evidence layers. Her method — following the material supply chain from mine to model — is a direct methodological model. The book also names what we're investigating: *the gap between AI's self-presentation and its actual planetary costs* is our primary object.
