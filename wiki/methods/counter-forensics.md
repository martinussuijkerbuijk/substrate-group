# Counter-Forensics

**Origin:** Weizman / Forensic Architecture. See [[forensic-architecture-violence]].

**Definition:** Using the evidentiary frameworks, standards, and forums of institutions against those same institutions to produce accountability for harm. Counter-forensics is not independent journalism — it produces evidence *in the forms that legal and paralegal forums recognize*, aimed at specific decision points (a court, a parliamentary inquiry, a UN mechanism, a procurement review).

**Key moves:**
1. **Identify the forum first** — what institution could act on this evidence, and what evidentiary standards does it require?
2. **Work backward from the standard** — what evidence would be sufficient for that forum, and can it be produced?
3. **Treat the threshold as the object** — don't just find evidence; analyze what threshold architecture keeps this harm below the level of institutional response
4. **Document negative evidence** — what would we expect to find if the harm was occurring, and is it absent? Redactions, NDAs, classification marks are themselves evidence

**Distinction from journalism:** Journalism asks "what happened?" Counter-forensics asks "what evidence, in what form, for which forum, at what moment, would produce accountability?" These are different questions. The team produces evidentiary resources, not news.
