# Four-Layer Evidence Analysis

**Origin:** Team method (TEAM.md). Derived from Weizman's counter-forensic multi-register approach and Star's ecology of infrastructure.

**The four layers:**

| Layer | Question | Lead |
|-------|----------|------|
| Observable Outputs | What decisions were made? What patterns appear? | Code Ethnographer |
| Material Infrastructure | What hardware runs the system? Where? At what ecological cost? | Material Ecologist |
| Contracts & Procurement | What was legally authorized? What oversight was waived? | Capital Analyst |
| Affected Community Testimony | What do people subject to the system report? How does it diverge? | Community Liaison |

**How to use:** Each layer is investigated autonomously. No layer waits for another. Convergence between layers elevates confidence level. Divergence between layers is documented as a Divergence Finding — it is not resolved, it is mapped. The Orchestrator convenes cross-layer synthesis after each agent has completed their autonomous phase.

**What this catches:** The gap between what the system *says it does* (observable outputs layer), what it *physically is* (material layer), what it *was authorized to do* (contracts layer), and what it *actually does to people* (testimony layer). All four rarely converge. The shape of the divergence is the primary finding.
