# Performed Transparency

**Source:** Concept introduced in [[eu-border-facial-recognition]] dossier; draws on [[opacity-architecture]], [[infrastructure-invisibility]]

**Definition:** The deployment of procedural compliance mechanisms — impact assessments, parliamentary scrutiny, regulatory opinions, public procurement notices — that produce an appearance of democratic oversight while the technical system expands its operational capacity. The transparency is staged; the oversight is always already too late, too narrow, or non-binding. The system performs being overseen so that it may continue operating without effective constraint.

**In our work:** Performed transparency is a mechanism of [[opacity-architecture]]. It is not the absence of oversight but its strategic simulation. When the team encounters a DPIA, a public consultation, or a transparency report, we ask: what does this document make visible, what does it make sayable, and what does it render impossible to ask? The performance of transparency often serves to legitimize systems that would otherwise face political resistance.
