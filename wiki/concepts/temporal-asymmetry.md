# Temporal Asymmetry

**Source:** Team concept; formalized in [[eu-border-facial-recognition]] dossier; draws on Stiegler's pharmacology of technics

**Definition:** The structural mismatch between the speed of system deployment and the speed of accountability; between the moment of data capture and the duration of retention; between the time of the investigation and the time of ongoing harm. The system acts in milliseconds; accountability operates in years. Decay time is always slower than capture time.

**In our work:** Temporal asymmetry is not a logistical problem but an epistemic and political one. It means that by the time an investigation produces knowledge, the system has already expanded. It means that data retained for five years outlives any appeal, any oversight mechanism, any political moment. The team tags every finding with temporal coordinates and refuses the fantasy of catching up. We are slow; harm is fast. This asymmetry is part of the architecture we study.
