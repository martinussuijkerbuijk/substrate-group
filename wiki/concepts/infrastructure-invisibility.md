# Infrastructure Invisibility

**Source:** [[star-ruhleder-1996]]

**Definition:** Infrastructure becomes visible only when it breaks. In normal operation, it is taken for granted — embedded, transparent, relied upon without notice. This invisibility is not accidental; it is a feature of how infrastructure is designed and maintained. Membership in a community of practice grants access to infrastructure; non-members encounter it as obstacle.

**In our work:** AI infrastructure is designed to be doubly invisible: technically (systems are opaque by architecture) and institutionally (classified contracts, proprietary systems, jurisdictional diffusion). The team treats the *design of invisibility* as a primary object of investigation, not just an obstacle to investigation. When infrastructure becomes briefly visible — through a failure, a leak, a lawsuit — those moments are primary evidence.
