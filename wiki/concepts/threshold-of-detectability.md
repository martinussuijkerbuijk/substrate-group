# Threshold of Detectability

**Source:** [[forensic-architecture-violence]]

**Definition:** The level below which an act of violence, harm, or illegality is calibrated to remain invisible to the sensors, legal standards, or institutional attention that would trigger accountability. Violence does not need to be invisible — it needs only to remain below the threshold at which response is activated. The threshold is itself a political instrument: it can be raised (by weakening oversight) or lowered (by strengthening evidence standards).

**In our work:** AI systems causing harm operate at or below thresholds of detection — statistical noise, plausible deniability, contractual opacity, jurisdictional diffusion. The team's task is not simply to find the harm, but to identify *what threshold architecture* keeps it undetectable. The Shape of the Gap analysis is a direct application of this concept.
