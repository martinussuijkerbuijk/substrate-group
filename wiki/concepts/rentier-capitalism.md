# Rentier Capitalism

**Source:** [[rentier-capitalism-christophers]]

**Definition:** An economic system organized primarily around the extraction of rent — returns derived from ownership of scarce assets rather than from productive activity. Asset classes include: land, intellectual property, financial instruments, platform infrastructure, natural resources, and data. Christophers argues the contemporary UK (and by extension, global tech) economy is characterized not by competitive markets but by monopolistic rent extraction.

**In our work:** AI systems are rentier infrastructure. Cloud providers, semiconductor manufacturers, and platform operators extract rent from a captive digital economy. Procurement contracts often lock public institutions into perpetual rent payments for systems they cannot exit. The Capital pillar maps these rent flows — who owns the chokepoints, and what contracts formalize the extraction.
