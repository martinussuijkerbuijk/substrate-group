# Situated Knowledge

**Source:** Haraway (referenced throughout team files)

**Definition:** All knowledge is produced from a specific location — embodied, partial, positioned. There is no view from nowhere. The claim to objectivity through detachment is itself a situated claim that conceals its location. Situated knowledge demands that the knower account for their position as part of the epistemic act.

**In our work:** Every dossier includes an explicit reflexive framing statement: who is investigating, from where, with what access, under what constraints. This is not a disclaimer — it is primary evidence about what can and cannot be known from this position. The team's institutional location (university-adjacent, Western, technologically capable) shapes what we can see and what we cannot.
