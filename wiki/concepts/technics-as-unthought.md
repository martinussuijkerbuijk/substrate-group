# Technics as the Unthought

**Source:** [[technics-and-time-stiegler]]

**Definition:** Stiegler's argument that Western philosophy has systematically repressed technics — the technical object, the tool, the prosthesis — as a mere instrument subordinate to the human subject. Technology is not applied science; it is constitutive of the human. The "fault of Epimetheus" is that the human animal arrived in the world without natural qualities and had to be given technics as supplement, making technics originary, not derivative.

**In our work:** AI is not a tool applied to pre-existing social problems. It is a technical object that constitutes new social arrangements, new temporalities, new forms of memory and forgetting (tertiary retention). The Code pillar investigates this: what new subject positions does an AI system create? What does it make possible to forget? The Philosophy Theorist applies Stiegler to diagnose what "AI governance" frameworks systematically leave unthought.
