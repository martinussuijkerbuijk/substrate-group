# Lawscape

**Source:** [[spatial-justice-philippopoulos]]

**Definition:** The tautological co-constitution of law and space — law is always spatial (it requires a territory, a body, a jurisdiction), and space is always legal (every space is claimed, regulated, contested). The lawscape is not a metaphor but a description of material reality: the borders that divide data centers, the contracts that define cloud regions, the permits that authorize extraction.

**In our work:** AI infrastructure is always a lawscape problem. Where is the server physically located? Whose law governs the contract? Who has standing to bring a claim? Jurisdictional arbitrage — deliberately locating infrastructure across multiple legal regimes to escape accountability — is a lawscape strategy. The Capital pillar maps the lawscape of each investigation.
