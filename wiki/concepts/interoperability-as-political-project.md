# Interoperability as Political Project

**Source:** Concept introduced in [[eu-border-facial-recognition]] dossier; draws on [[lawscape]], [[boundary-objects]]

**Definition:** The technical and legal work of connecting previously separate databases so that data captured for one purpose becomes available for another. Interoperability is never merely technical; it restructures the legal and political meaning of the data and the bodily status of the person to whom it refers. It transforms "asylum data" into "border risk data" through the architecture of connection.

**In our work:** Interoperability is how surveillance scales without new legislation. By connecting existing systems, states create capabilities that no single authorization would have permitted. The team treats interoperability as a political technology: the "European Search Portal" or "Common Identity Repository" are boundary objects that appear technical to engineers and legal to lawyers, but are fundamentally about redefining who can be known and how.
