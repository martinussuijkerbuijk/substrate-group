# Distributed Presence

**Source:** Team concept; formalized in [[eu-border-facial-recognition]] dossier; draws on actor-network theory and [[lawscape]]

**Definition:** The architectural dispersal of a technical system across multiple jurisdictions, legal regimes, and physical sites so that no single location or actor can be held accountable for the whole. The "system" exists as a network effect rather than as a discrete object. Its presence is everywhere and nowhere.

**In our work:** Distributed presence is how infrastructure escapes responsibility. A face is captured at one coordinate, processed at another, stored at a third, and matched against a fourth — each step governed by a different legal regime, owned by a different entity, maintained by a different workforce. The team does not treat the "system" as a bounded object but traces the connections that make it cohere. Where is the facial recognition system? The answer is: in the relation between the nodes, not in any node itself.
