# Biometric Substrate

**Source:** Concept introduced in [[eu-border-facial-recognition]] dossier; draws on [[technics-as-unthought]], [[situated-knowledge]]

**Definition:** The ontological claim that the body produces stable, machine-readable identity data that precedes and transcends political context. The face, fingerprint, or iris is treated as a raw substrate from which truth about identity, risk, and eligibility can be algorithmically extracted. This claim erases the relational, historical, and political constitution of identity.

**In our work:** The biometric substrate is the unthought of border AI. It allows a facial image captured at a border to be treated as "identity itself" rather than as a situated, partial, and politically produced representation. The team asks: what is lost when a face becomes a template? What labor of extraction, categorization, and comparison is hidden by the term "recognition"? We treat the body not as a source of raw data but as a site of political contestation.
