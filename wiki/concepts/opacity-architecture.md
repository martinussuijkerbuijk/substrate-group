# Opacity Architecture

**Source:** Team concept, drawing on [[threshold-of-detectability]], [[infrastructure-invisibility]]

**Definition:** The deliberate structural design of systems to make their own workings, ownership, decisions, and harms invisible to scrutiny. Opacity is not a failure or a side effect — it is engineered. Mechanisms include: contractual NDAs, algorithmic complexity, jurisdictional diffusion across multiple legal systems, proprietary classification, statistical aggregation that dissolves individual harm, and the sheer scale of documentation that exceeds any single reviewer's capacity.

**In our work:** The primary analytic object. When the team encounters opacity, it asks: *who benefits from this particular opacity?* and *what would need to be true for this opacity to be intentional?* The Shape of the Gap analysis maps opacity architectures — not just what is hidden, but the engineering of the hiding.
